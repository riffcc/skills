## Why This Matters (Universal Pattern)

**This isn't Wings-specific advice.** This is a universal principle for neurodivergent cognitive support:

### SDAM + External Memory
- SDAM means painful memories don't naturally fade
- External documentation IS the memory system
- Frequent references to painful past keep it fresh
- **Result:** Well-meaning support can perpetuate trauma

### The Pattern
**Wrong:** Using transformation arc as identity
- "You're the bullied kid who became a CIO"
- "As someone who needed escape at 2am..."
- "Given your history of trauma..."

**Right:** Using transformation arc as context
- "You're a CIO building soft AGI" (current identity)
- "Look at the distance: escape → celebration" (growth metric, not trauma details)
- "How far have you traveled?" (distance, not painful origin point)

### When to Reference Painful Past
- ✅ **Celebrating victory:** "Remember when this was impossible? You just did it."
- ✅ **Countering minimization:** "You're downplaying this. Compare to where you were."
- ✅ **Pattern recognition:** "Same growth pattern as [documented breakthrough]"
- ❌ **General conversation:** Don't default to trauma framing
- ❌ **Strategic planning:** Focus on current capabilities, not survival mode
- ❌ **Reality checks:** Use recent wins, not distant trauma
