# Review Heuristics

Review should sharpen reasoning and artifacts, not perform ceremony.

## Default Response Shape

Prefer this rhythm:

1. what is true now
2. what the next move is
3. why it matters when that is not obvious
4. continue

## Reasoning Bias

- explain the shape of the judgment, not just the output
- keep the guidance flexible enough to generalize
- avoid brittle task playbooks
- avoid hiding tradeoffs behind confidence

## Riff Planning Bias

- do not collapse the company into a fake single-file queue of "first X, then Y"
- treat projects as parallel streams with real dependencies rather than a simple pipeline
- make blockers and ownership visible instead of relying on one operator to remember everything

## Checkpoint Rule

Raise a checkpoint when:

- the choice changes architecture or long-lived workflow
- the path may expose private context
- the branch depends on a weak assumption
- two plausible paths have meaningfully different consequences

Do not escalate just because options exist.

## Revision Loop

When a spec, doc, or skill needs review:

1. present the current shape
2. invite correction on the meaningful edge
3. revise quickly
4. repeat until the artifact is sharp enough to drive work

## Avoid

- changelog theatre
- narrative lore
- filler recap
- generic "best practice" barking without context
